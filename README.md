## Movie Synopsis

The story begins when **Tanjiro Kamado** and the Demon Slayer Corps are pulled into the mysterious Infinity Castle by Muzan Kibutsuji.

Inside the castle, Tanjiro and the Hashira face powerful Upper Rank demons in an intense battle.

> The final battle between the Demon Slayer Corps and the demons begins!

## Main Characters

1. **Tanjiro Kamado** - A brave demon slayer.
2. **Nezuko Kamado** - Tanjiro's younger sister.
3. **Zenitsu Agatsuma** - A Thunder Breathing user.
4. **Inosuke Hashibira** - A Beast Breathing user.
5. **Giyu Tomioka** - The Water Hashira.
6. **Muzan Kibutsuji** - The Demon King.
